# 👥 HR Analytics & Employee Attrition Dashboard

An interactive **HR Analytics Dashboard** built using **Microsoft Power BI** to analyze employee attrition, workforce demographics, salary distribution, job roles, education background, age groups, and employee tenure.

The dashboard provides HR teams and business stakeholders with a consolidated view of employee attrition patterns and helps identify the major factors associated with employee turnover.

---

## 📊 Project Overview

Employee attrition is an important HR metric that can significantly affect an organization's productivity, hiring costs, and workforce stability.

This project uses **Power BI** to transform employee data into an interactive HR analytics dashboard.

The dashboard allows users to analyze attrition based on:

* Gender
* Education
* Age
* Salary Slab
* Years at Company
* Job Role

It also provides key workforce KPIs such as employee count, total attrition, attrition rate, average age, average salary, and average years at the company.

---

## 📈 Dashboard Analysis

### 1. 👨‍💼 Attrition by Gender

The dashboard compares employee attrition between male and female employees.

| Gender | Attrition |
| ------ | --------: |
| Male   |   **140** |
| Female |    **79** |

This visualization helps HR teams compare attrition patterns across gender groups.

---

### 2. 🎓 Attrition by Education

The dashboard analyzes employee attrition across different educational backgrounds.

The major education categories shown include:

* Life Sciences
* Medical
* Marketing
* Technical
* Other

The displayed distribution includes:

| Education Field |   Share |
| --------------- | ------: |
| Life Sciences   | **38%** |
| Medical         | **27%** |
| Marketing       | **15%** |
| Technical       | **14%** |
| Other           |  **5%** |

**Key observation:** Life Sciences represents the largest share in the displayed education analysis.

---

### 3. 🎂 Attrition by Age

The dashboard groups employee attrition into different age ranges.

| Age Group | Attrition |
| --------- | --------: |
| 26–35     |   **116** |
| 18–25     |    **44** |
| 36–45     |    **43** |
| 46–55     |    **26** |
| 55+       |     **8** |

**Key observation:** The **26–35 age group** has the highest attrition in the dashboard.

---

### 4. 💰 Attrition by Salary Slab

The dashboard analyzes attrition based on salary ranges.

| Salary Slab | Attrition |
| ----------- | --------: |
| Up to 5K    |   **163** |
| 5K–10K      |    **49** |
| 10K–15K     |    **20** |
| 15K+        |     **5** |

**Key observation:** Employees in the **Up to 5K** salary slab account for the largest share of attrition.

This can help HR teams investigate whether compensation is associated with employee turnover.

---

### 5. ⏳ Attrition by Years at Company

The dashboard analyzes employee attrition according to the number of years employees have spent with the organization.

The visualization helps identify:

* Early-stage employee turnover
* Employees with longer tenure
* Attrition patterns across different tenure levels
* Potential retention issues

The dashboard indicates a particularly high attrition point around the early years of employment.
